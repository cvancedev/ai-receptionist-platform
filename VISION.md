# Project Vision

## Purpose

Help small businesses never miss another customer.

## Mission

Empower small businesses with dependable AI employees that answer customers, capture opportunities, reduce repetitive work, and help businesses grow confidently.

## Initial Product

The first product is an AI receptionist.

Version 1 will remain intentionally focused. It will not become a general CRM, accounting platform, dispatch platform, or all-in-one business suite.

## Long-Term Vision

The company may eventually offer additional AI employees, such as:

- AI Scheduler
- AI Follow-Up Assistant
- AI Sales Assistant
- AI Office Manager

These are future possibilities, not current development commitments.

## Core Values

- Trust
- Reliability
- Simplicity
- Service
- Security
- Accessibility
- Maintainability
- Genuine customer value

## Product Promise

Never miss another customer.

## Founding Principle

This project will be built like a real software company, not a coding exercise.

Every decision should improve at least one of these areas:

- Customer value
- Product quality
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Performance
- Security
- User experience

If a proposal does not clearly improve one of those areas, it should not be implemented.

“We are building something our future selves will be proud to own, our customers will be proud to recommend, and our team will be proud to maintain.”
