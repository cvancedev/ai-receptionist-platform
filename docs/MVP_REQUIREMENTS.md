# MVP Requirements

## Executive Summary

The MVP is a dependable AI receptionist for inquiry-driven service businesses. It should respond to a new inquiry, understand the customer's need, gather complete universal and service-specific information, confirm accuracy, produce a useful summary, explain an approved next step, and hand judgment back to human staff.

The MVP exists to help a business answer every inquiry, capture every opportunity, and reduce repetitive intake work. It must help customers feel heard without overpromising, applying pressure, or replacing human judgment.

## Problem Statement

Small service-business teams cannot always respond while working, traveling, or helping another customer. When the first response is delayed or incomplete, important details and follow-up opportunities can be missed.

## Target Customer

The initial target is an owner or manager of a small, inquiry-driven service business, with moving-related businesses as the first discovery segment. The target team handles recurring customer questions and needs a clear human handoff from each conversation.

## Core Value Proposition

Provide a consistent first response that captures the customer's request and gives human staff a complete, readable next step—without pretending the AI can make business decisions.

## Must-Have Features

- A professional greeting and clear understanding of the customer's request
- Universal intake for customer, project, location, timing, communication, and notes
- A limited service-specific information layer
- Natural confirmation and correction of captured details
- A structured, readable inquiry summary
- An approved explanation of next steps
- Clear handling of uncertainty and human escalation
- Guardrails against invented answers, pressure, and unsupported promises
- A dependable handoff that preserves customer context for staff

## Future Features

- Automated scheduling
- Automated follow-up
- Calendar and business-system integrations
- Additional communication channels
- Additional validated service categories
- Multilingual experiences
- Reporting and analytics after a measurement and privacy plan exists

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Replacing human judgment or customer relationships
- Providing autonomous quotes, binding prices, contracts, or guarantees
- Promising availability, arrival times, or service outcomes without approval
- Processing payments
- Acting as a general CRM, dispatch, accounting, or all-in-one operations platform
- Building detailed service questionnaires before Milestone 2.2 validation
- Implementing AI, APIs, authentication, storage, forms, or dashboards in this architecture milestone

## Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when customer and business testing shows that it can:

- Capture required universal information and relevant service context consistently
- Let customers correct misunderstandings before the conversation ends
- Produce a summary that staff can act on without reconstructing the conversation
- Explain next steps without unsupported commitments
- Escalate uncertainty and judgment to a person
- Reduce repetitive intake effort while maintaining a respectful customer experience
- Earn validation from service-business owners before the product scope expands
