# Service Types

## Purpose

The first service taxonomy keeps the universal conversation consistent while identifying the limited context that differs by service. These categories are starting points for validation, not a commitment to support every category in the first release.

Detailed questionnaires are intentionally deferred to Milestone 2.2.

## Moving

Unique information may include:

- Origin and destination when known
- Move date or timing flexibility
- Approximate home or project size
- Access constraints such as stairs, elevators, parking, or long carries
- Items or circumstances requiring special handling
- Whether packing, storage, or labor-only help may also be needed

## Junk Removal

Unique information may include:

- Pickup location
- General item types and approximate volume
- Access constraints
- Materials that may require special handling or may not be accepted
- Preferred removal timing

## Packing

Unique information may include:

- Areas, rooms, or items to be packed
- Whether materials are needed
- Fragile, valuable, or unusually sized items
- Required completion date
- Whether packing is connected to a move or is a standalone service

## Labor Only

Unique information may include:

- Work the crew is expected to perform
- Location and access conditions
- Approximate scope or duration
- Equipment or vehicle responsibilities
- Timing and coordination constraints

## Storage

Unique information may include:

- Approximate amount or size of stored items
- Expected storage duration
- Move-in timing
- Expected access frequency
- Climate, handling, or other special concerns

## Other

Unique information may include:

- The customer's desired outcome
- Relevant locations and timing
- Important constraints or concerns
- Enough description for human staff to classify the request

The receptionist should not force an uncertain request into the wrong category. “Other” should preserve the opportunity and support a human decision.
